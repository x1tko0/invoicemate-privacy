InvoiceMate

PDF Maker — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your documents stay on your phone. InvoiceMate has no accounts, no cloud, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Data the app stores

What you enter: your business details (name, tax ID, address, contacts, bank details, payment link), clients, catalog items, documents and their line items, your logo, signature and stamp images, and app settings.

  • Stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Sharing, saving and backups

  • PDFs and backups leave the app only when you choose to share, save, print or back them up, to the app or folder you pick.
  • Android's own system backup may include the app's data if you enabled it on your phone; this is controlled by you and Google, not by the app.
  • Images (logo, stamp) are picked with the system photo picker; the app has no access to your other photos.

Purchases

InvoiceMate Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are seen only by Google. The app only checks whether Pro was bought.

Notifications

Optional overdue-payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data.
